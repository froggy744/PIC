BrowseScope

sidebar filter · search · rating · metadata filters · sort

predicate() facet Scope is_narrowed() admits(photo)
seam: db queries take a BrowseScope

Problem. Browsing state lives in settings rows and two cells. 7 db functions read it implicitly. The rating is applied in SQL and again in Rust. The “is the view narrowed?” check exists in two versions: the one used by 5 call sites ignores the rating. Suspected bugs: sidebar Ratings with the toolbar on Unrated comes out empty; the scan append path adds new photos to a rating-filtered view.

Solution. One BrowseScope value, loaded once and passed into the queries. The facet scope, the narrowed check and “does this new photo belong here?” all derive from it.

  • locality: one rating rule
  • leverage: one value, ~40 call sites
  • no hidden settings reads
  • tests: table over scope × photos

Problem. The Albums home is fed by the filtered list from 6 sites and by the unfiltered list from album edits and Smart Album recounts. Suspected bug: after any album edit, the home's counts stop following the active filters. The sidebar has 5 writers, and the home is reached through a slot closure cloned 4+ times.

Solution. An AlbumList module: callers say “albums changed”, and it picks the right query per view, runs it once (off-thread) and writes every album surface.
